# Privacy Policy for AI E-Commerce Empire

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

Welcome to **AI E-Commerce Empire** ("the App"), developed by Tshiamo Jantjie ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparent information regarding our data practices.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Philosophy
AI E-Commerce Empire is an interactive simulation and analytics environment designed for e-commerce store telemetry, dynamic pricing simulations, inventory forecasting, and revenue pipeline intelligence.

**Key Commitment:** All business telemetry, catalog data, pricing rules, and dashboard configurations generated within the App are processed and retained **locally on your device** (e.g., via browser `localStorage` or client sandboxing). We do not operate proprietary tracking servers to collect your personal identity or commercial transaction data.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Identification Information (PII):** We do not require you to create an account, register, or disclose your name, email address, physical address, or phone number to operate the core application.
- **Financial & Payment Data:** The revenue values, pricing plans, and checkout metrics displayed inside the App are purely simulated or user-provided parameters. We do not collect credit card credentials, bank account numbers, or real-money transaction keys.
- **Sensitive Device Permissions:** The App does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, GPS location, photos, or media libraries.

---

### 3. Automatically Collected Technical Data
When running as an installed mobile application through standard app distribution platforms (such as the Google Play Store):
- **Diagnostic and Performance Metrics:** Anonymous crash logs, app execution latency, and device hardware specifications (e.g., OS version, device model) may be aggregated by platform tools (such as Google Play Console) to diagnose performance bottlenecks and resolve bugs.
- **Advertising / Analytics Identifiers:** If optional third-party advertising or analytics SDKs are incorporated into packaged builds, non-sensitive identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) may be utilized for frequency capping, attribution, and aggregate metrics in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

---

### 4. Data Storage & User Control
- **Local Persistence:** Your product catalogs, demand surges, simulated orders, and customized revenue goals are stored directly within your device's local application cache.
- **Data Deletion:** You retain complete control over your data. You may purge all saved store metrics, catalog settings, and simulation logs at any time by clearing the application's cache/storage through your device's **Settings > Apps > AI E-Commerce Empire > Storage > Clear Data**, or by uninstalling the application.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
AI E-Commerce Empire does not knowingly target, collect, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove any such data.

---

### 6. Security
We implement industry-standard safeguards to maintain the integrity of client-side operations. Because data remains localized to your device storage, maintaining device security and screen locking helps protect your local records.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or evolving regulatory obligations. Material modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
